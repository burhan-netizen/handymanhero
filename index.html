Is my job too small?

No. Small jobs are exactly why Handyman Hero started. A single fixture, a stuck door or a whole page of little fixes are all welcome. Bundle a few together and it all gets done in one visit.

Are estimates really free?

Yes. Send your list or describe the project and you'll get a clear price for labor and materials before any work begins. No pressure, no obligation.

Do you take on bigger projects?

Yes. Bathroom and kitchen updates, decks, porches, basement finishing, LVP flooring and exterior painting are all on the table. For anything that needs a licensed specialist, Christopher works with trusted local pros.

How soon can you come out?

Work is booked by appointment, Monday to Friday, 8 AM to 5 PM. Call or send the form and you'll hear back the same business day with the next available time.

Can you look after my property while I'm away?

Yes. House sitting, property monitoring and basic property management are available for snowbirds, landlords and anyone away for a while.
